## ABOUT THIS FAQ

This document answers common questions about using the Online Auction System.

Answers are based on confirmed business policies and the original Software Requirements Specification.

Where a policy is awaiting approval, this FAQ explicitly identifies it as pending approval. It must not be interpreted as an implemented or officially approved feature.

# A. GENERAL

### Q1. What is the Online Auction System?

The Online Auction System is a platform where sellers list goods for auction and eligible users participate by placing bids.

The platform manages auction timing, bid validation, winner determination, and the defined credit and payment workflow.

### Q2. Can I browse auctions without logging in?

Yes.

Guests may browse and search auctions, view auction details, bid history, and comments.

However, logging in is required for actions such as bidding and commenting.

### Q3. Does the platform handle shipping or delivery?

No.

The platform does not provide shipping, delivery, or fulfillment services.

Buyers and sellers must arrange these services directly with one another.

You may contact the platform to request available information, but the platform does not provide or guarantee fulfillment services.

### Q4. What currency does the platform use?

The final supported currency has not yet been confirmed.

USD is currently used in the policy examples and proposed bid increment schedule.

Please refer to the approved platform terms for the supported currency.

# B. ACCOUNT AND SECURITY

### Q5. How do I create an account?

Register using the information requested by the system, including:

- Full name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Address.
- Password.

### Q6. Do I need to activate my account?

Yes.

You must complete the required email activation process before using account features that require an activated account.

### Q7. What are the password requirements?

The password must contain at least eight characters and include letters and numbers.

### Q8. What happens if I leave my account inactive?

Your session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.

You must log in again to continue using authenticated features.

### Q9. How can I change or reset my password?

To change your password while logged in, use the password-change feature and provide your current password.

If you forget your password, use the password-reset process and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

# C. CREDIT ACCOUNT AND PAYMENTS

### Q10. When is my credit account created?

Your credit account is created after successful account activation.

The initial credit balance is zero.

### Q11. How do I add credit?

The original system requirements specify that users contact platform staff to deposit funds.

The final top-up process and supported payment methods must be confirmed in the approved platform instructions.

### Q12. Do I have to authorize a payment method?

Yes.

You must authorize the supported payment provider or payment method to perform the payment operations required by the platform.

This includes credit top-ups and any permitted withdrawals.

### Q13. What happens if my payment fails?

A failed payment must be rolled back.

This rule also applies to a failed winner-payment transaction.

The system must not leave an incomplete debit, credit, or settlement state.

### Q14. Does the system reserve my credit when I place a bid?

The recommended design is to reserve the amount of your current leading bid.

Under this proposed design:

- Your credit is reserved when you become the highest bidder.
- Your previous reservation is released if another user outbids you.
- The winning reservation is converted into a payment debit after successful settlement.
- Other reservations are released when the auction closes.

This reservation lifecycle is pending final approval.

### Q15. Can my credit be locked?

Yes.

If the system detects suspicious or prohibited activity, the platform may restrict your account and temporarily lock or hold associated credit while the activity is reviewed.

The final investigation, notification, appeal, and credit-release procedures require approval.

# D. SELLING AND LISTING GOODS

### Q16. How do I create an auction?

You must add your goods before creating an auction.

The auction must contain the required information, including:

- Auction name.
- Description.
- Goods.
- Initial price.
- Reserve price.
- Start date and time.
- Auction duration.

### Q17. Can I edit or delete goods after listing them?

Goods may only be edited or deleted when they are not associated with an active auction or a completed sale.

### Q18. Can I cancel my auction?

A seller may cancel an auction only before it starts.

After cancellation, the auction is no longer presented as an active auction.

### Q19. Am I responsible for the accuracy of my listing?

Yes.

Sellers are responsible for providing accurate goods information, including the item's description, images, identity, and condition.

### Q20. Does the platform deliver the item to the winner?

No.

The platform does not provide shipping, delivery, or fulfillment services.

The buyer and seller must arrange delivery, shipping, collection, and other fulfillment activities directly.

# E. BIDDING

### Q21. Who can place a bid?

A logged-in and eligible user may place a bid.

The project requirements also specify that a user must join or participate in an active auction before bidding, where that participation workflow applies.

### Q22. Can I place more than one bid in the same auction?

Yes.

You may place multiple valid bids in the same auction, provided that each bid satisfies the bidding rules and available-credit checks.

### Q23. Can I bid on my own auction?

No.

Self-bidding is strictly prohibited.

This includes bidding through another account or using another arrangement intended to manipulate your own auction.

### Q24. What happens if I engage in self-bidding or suspicious activity?

The platform may:

- Restrict your account.
- Suspend your account.
- Permanently ban your account.
- Temporarily lock or hold your associated credit while the activity is investigated.

### Q25. How much higher must my bid be?

The minimum increment depends on the current highest valid bid.

| Current Highest Bid | Minimum Increment |
|---|---:|
| $1.00 to below $10.00 | $0.10 |
| $10.00 to below $100.00 | $1.00 |
| $100.00 through $1,000.00 inclusive | $10.00 |
| Above $1,000.00 | $100.00 |

The increment for a current highest bid below $1.00 has not been confirmed.

A $0.01 increment has been suggested but is not yet an approved rule.

### Q26. What happens if another user outbids me?

Your bid is no longer the highest valid bid.

Under the recommended credit reservation design, the reservation associated with your previous leading bid is released.

The credit reservation design is pending final approval.

### Q27. Does the auction automatically extend when someone bids near the end?

A two-minute soft-close extension has been recommended.

Under this proposal:

- A valid bid received during the final two minutes extends the auction by another two minutes.
- Another qualifying bid during the extended final two-minute period triggers another extension.

This feature is pending approval and must not be treated as active until it appears in the approved auction rules.

# F. AUCTION CLOSING AND WINNER DETERMINATION

### Q28. Who wins an auction?

The bidder with the highest valid bid wins only if that bid is equal to or greater than the seller's reserve price.

The highest bid alone does not guarantee a win.

### Q29. What is a reserve price?

A reserve price is the minimum price the seller requires for the item to be sold.

The highest valid bid must reach or exceed the reserve price for the auction to have a winner.

### Q30. What happens if the highest bid is below the reserve price?

The auction closes as `UNSOLD`.

In this situation:

- No winner is declared.
- No winner payment is processed.
- No seller proceeds are generated.
- All related credit reservations are released.

### Q31. What happens if nobody places a valid bid?

The auction closes as `UNSOLD`.

No winner payment or seller proceeds are generated, and any related credit reservations are released.

### Q32. What happens after I win?

The system processes the winner-payment transaction.

If the payment succeeds, the seller's proceeds are calculated after deducting the applicable platform commission.

If the payment fails, it must be rolled back like any other failed payment.

### Q33. Can I get a refund after winning?

Completed auction sales are final.

As the winner, you are responsible for your purchase. You cannot request a refund merely because:

- You changed your mind.
- You no longer want the item.
- You decide not to complete the purchase for personal reasons.

This policy does not override mandatory legal rights, legally required remedies, or corrections required because of fraud, unauthorized transactions, or platform processing errors.

The final exceptions and procedures require legal and business approval.

# G. PLATFORM COMMISSION

### Q34. How much commission does the platform charge?

The source requirements specify the following seller commission schedule:

| Winning Amount | Platform Commission |
|---|---:|
| Below $100 | 10% |
| $100 through $999 | 5% |
| $1,000 through $9,999 | 2% |
| Above $10,000 | 1% |

The exact treatment of a winning amount of $10,000, the final supported currency, and the commission rounding method still require confirmation.

# H. DISPUTES, SERVICE LIMITATIONS, AND SUPPORT

### Q35. What should buyers and sellers do if there is a dispute?

Both parties must understand and acknowledge the platform's service scope and limitations before a dispute case is handed off or escalated.

They should first attempt to resolve the issue through the available support or communication process.

If the dispute cannot be resolved, it may be referred to the competent court under applicable law.

The intended jurisdictional reference is the court with jurisdiction where the item is held. The exact legal wording and enforceability require legal review.

### Q36. Does the platform resolve every dispute?

The platform provides only the services defined in its terms.

The platform does not provide shipping, delivery, or fulfillment services.

The precise scope of dispute assistance, evidence handling, and escalation must be defined in the approved terms.

### Q37. Can my account be suspended or banned?

Yes.

Prohibited user behavior may result in account suspension or a permanent ban.

Examples include:

- Self-bidding.
- Bid manipulation.
- Fraudulent activities.
- Suspicious or unnatural bidding behavior.
- Other prohibited conduct.

### Q38. Can I appeal an account restriction?

An appeal process is recommended, but the exact procedure, deadlines, required evidence, and review process have not yet been approved.

Contact platform support for the currently available procedure.
