# 2. CREDIT ACCOUNT AND PAYMENT POLICIES

### POL-CRE-001 — Credit Account Creation

A credit account is created after successful account activation.

The initial credit balance is zero.

The original requirements specify that users contact platform staff to deposit funds. The final top-up workflow must be documented separately.

**Source:** Defined by SRS.

### POL-CRE-002 — Payment Authorization

**CONFIRMED**

Users must authorize the supported payment provider or payment method to perform the payment operations required by the platform.

This includes:

- Adding credit to their account.
- Authorizing permitted withdrawals.
- Completing payment operations associated with auction settlement.

Payment authorization must be obtained through the supported payment process.

### POL-CRE-003 — Failed Payment Rollback

**CONFIRMED**

Every failed payment must be rolled back.

A failed winner-payment transaction follows exactly the same rollback requirement as any other failed payment.

The system must ensure that a failed transaction does not leave:

- An incomplete debit.
- An incomplete credit.
- An incorrect account balance.
- An incomplete seller payment.
- An inconsistent auction settlement state.

A failed transaction must not be treated as a successful payment.

### POL-CRE-004 — Credit Reservation and Release

**RECOMMENDATION — PENDING APPROVAL**

The system should reserve credit when a bidder becomes the highest bidder.

Recommended rules:

1. Before accepting a bid, the system verifies that the bidder has sufficient available credit.
2. When the bidder becomes the highest bidder, the system reserves the amount of their current leading bid.
3. When another bidder outbids them, the previous reservation is released.
4. The new highest bidder's current leading bid is reserved.
5. When the auction closes successfully, the winner's reservation is converted into the final debit.
6. Reservations belonging to other bidders are released.
7. If an auction is cancelled or cannot be settled, all related reservations are released.

Recommended calculation:

`Available Credit = Posted Credit Balance - Active Credit Reservations`

Reservations should be tracked independently from the posted credit balance.

### POL-CRE-005 — Credit Restrictions

**CONFIRMED**

If the system detects suspicious or prohibited activity, the platform may restrict the user's account and temporarily lock or hold associated credit while the activity is reviewed.

The platform must follow applicable laws and its approved account-enforcement procedures.

### POL-CRE-006 — Payment Security

**RECOMMENDATION**

The platform should use a supported payment provider for payment authorization and processing.

The application should not store raw card security codes (CVV/CVC) or unnecessary payment credentials.

# 4. AUCTION CREATION AND LIFECYCLE

### POL-AUC-001 — Auction Creation

A seller must add goods before creating an auction.

An auction must contain the required information:

- Auction name.
- Description.
- Goods.
- Initial price.
- Reserve price.
- Start date and time.
- Auction duration.

**Source:** Defined by SRS.

### POL-AUC-002 — Automatic Auction Lifecycle

The system automatically starts and closes auctions according to their configured start and end times.

The backend must evaluate auctions using due-time conditions rather than relying on exact timestamp equality.

### POL-AUC-003 — Seller Cancellation

A seller may cancel an auction only before it starts.

After cancellation, the auction must no longer be presented as an active auction.

**Source:** Defined by SRS.

### POL-AUC-004 — Reserve Price

**CONFIRMED**

The reserve price is retained as a mandatory auction settlement condition.

The highest valid bid wins only if it is equal to or greater than the seller's reserve price.

The system must not declare a winner merely because a bidder has the highest bid.

### POL-AUC-005 — Unsold Auction

**CONFIRMED**

An auction must be marked as `UNSOLD` when:

- No valid bids were submitted before the auction closed; or
- The highest valid bid is lower than the reserve price.

For an unsold auction:

- No winner payment is processed.
- No seller proceeds are generated.
- All credit reservations associated with the auction must be released.

### POL-AUC-006 — Anti-Sniping / Soft-Close

**RECOMMENDATION — PENDING APPROVAL**

A soft-close mechanism is recommended.

Under this proposal:

- A valid bid received during the final two minutes extends the auction by another two minutes.
- If another qualifying bid is received during the new final two-minute window, the auction is extended again.
- The auction closes after the final extension period expires without another qualifying bid.

This behavior must be approved before being treated as an active system policy.

# 5. BIDDING RULES

### POL-BID-001 — Bidder Authentication

Only logged-in and eligible users may place bids.

**Source:** Defined by SRS.

### POL-BID-002 — Auction Participation

**PROJECT REQUIREMENT**

A user must join or participate in an active auction before placing a bid, where the system's participation workflow requires it.

### POL-BID-003 — Valid Bid

A bid must exceed the current highest valid bid by at least the applicable minimum bid increment.

The backend must validate every bid.

Frontend validation alone is insufficient.

### POL-BID-004 — Multiple Bids

A bidder may place multiple valid bids in the same auction, subject to:

- Bid increment requirements.
- Auction status.
- Available credit.
- Account eligibility.
- Other applicable bidding policies.

**Source:** Defined by SRS.

### POL-BID-005 — Self-Bidding Prohibition

**CONFIRMED**

Self-bidding is strictly prohibited.

A seller must not bid on their own auction, either directly or through another account or arrangement intended to manipulate the auction.

### POL-BID-006 — Suspicious Activity and Bid Manipulation

**CONFIRMED**

The platform prohibits suspicious or unnatural activities, including:

- Self-bidding.
- Bid manipulation.
- Artificially increasing auction prices.
- Fraudulent bidding.
- Collusion intended to manipulate auction outcomes.
- Using multiple accounts to manipulate bidding activities.

If the system detects suspicious or prohibited activity, the platform may:

- Restrict the account.
- Suspend the account.
- Permanently ban the account.
- Temporarily lock or hold associated credit while the activity is investigated.

The platform may take action according to its approved enforcement procedures and applicable laws.

### POL-BID-007 — Minimum Bid Increment Schedule

**CONFIRMED**

The minimum increment depends on the current highest valid bid.

| Current Highest Bid | Minimum Increment |
|---|---:|
| $1.00 to below $10.00 | $0.10 |
| $10.00 to below $100.00 | $1.00 |
| $100.00 through $1,000.00 inclusive | $10.00 |
| Above $1,000.00 | $100.00 |

The increment for a current highest bid below $1.00 has not yet been specified.

A $0.01 increment is suggested, but it is not a confirmed policy.

# 6. AUCTION SETTLEMENT AND WINNER DETERMINATION

### POL-SET-001 — Winner Determination

**CONFIRMED**

When an auction closes, the system identifies the highest valid bid.

The bidder with the highest valid bid wins only if:

`Highest Valid Bid >= Reserve Price`

The reserve-price condition must be evaluated by the backend during settlement.

### POL-SET-002 — Auction Without a Qualifying Winner

An auction must close as `UNSOLD` if:

- There are no valid bids; or
- The highest valid bid is below the reserve price.

For an unsold auction:

- No winner is declared.
- No winner debit is processed.
- No seller proceeds are generated.
- All related credit reservations are released.

### POL-SET-003 — Successful Settlement

When an auction is sold:

1. The system identifies the winning bidder.
2. The system processes the winner-payment transaction.
3. The applicable platform commission is calculated.
4. The seller receives the winning amount after the applicable commission is deducted.

### POL-SET-004 — Atomic Settlement

**RECOMMENDATION**

Winner debit, seller proceeds, commission, reservation conversion, reservation release, and settlement status should be processed atomically or through an idempotent settlement workflow.

A failed settlement must not result in duplicate charges or incomplete financial records.

# 7. REFUND POLICY

### POL-REF-001 — Final Sale and Refund Restrictions

**CONFIRMED**

Completed auction sales are final.

The winner takes responsibility for the purchase and is not entitled to a refund merely because they:

- Change their mind.
- No longer want the item.
- Decide they do not want to complete the purchase for personal reasons.

The platform does not voluntarily provide refunds for completed sales under this policy.

**LEGAL QUALIFICATION**

This policy does not override:

- Mandatory consumer-protection laws.
- Applicable auction laws.
- Court orders.
- Payment-provider requirements.
- Legally required remedies.
- Corrections required because of fraud, unauthorized transactions, or platform processing errors.

The final refund exceptions and procedures must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.

# 10. DISPUTES AND SERVICE LIMITATIONS

### POL-DIS-001 — Acknowledgement of Platform Service Boundaries

**CONFIRMED**

Before a dispute case is handed off or escalated, both the seller and bidder must understand and acknowledge the scope and limitations of the services provided by the platform.

### POL-DIS-002 — Attempted Dispute Resolution

Both parties should first attempt to resolve the dispute through the platform's available support or communication process.

### POL-DIS-003 — Unresolved Disputes and Court Jurisdiction

**CONFIRMED INTENT — LEGAL WORDING PENDING**

If a dispute cannot be resolved through the available process, it may be referred to the competent court under applicable law.

The product owner's intended jurisdictional reference is the court with jurisdiction in the location where the item is held.

The governing law, court venue, and enforceability of this clause must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.

### POL-DIS-004 — Platform Responsibility

The platform facilitates online auction activities within its defined service scope.

The platform does not provide shipping, delivery, or fulfillment services.

The final terms must clearly define which services the platform provides and which responsibilities remain with buyers and sellers.
